# [seedletimer.nl](https://seedletimer.nl/)
🪻 a timer app to help get productive using the pomodoro method combined with gamification. 🌱

improve your focus by forcing yourself to have some rest, and get rewarded for the time you've been productive by growing a garden! the simple design should help you to not get distracted and pay attention to your work. 🌱🪴

# how does it work?
it's very easy to start using seedle:
1. go to the website [https://seedletimer.nl/](https://seedletimer.nl/)
2. click on the start button
3. do some work during the focus session
4. take some time to get your mind off your work: stretch, look out the window, write down what's on your mind.
5. repeat!

# planned features
- a user profile with statistics, so you can analyse your productivity
- flowers stay in the garden for 4 days, it's planned for them to count towards unlocking new flower species
- a place to write down the things that are on your mind that are distracting you
- customizable things you should do during rests: stretch, look out the window, write down what's on your mind
- customizable rewards for completing a cycle (a group of sessions): watching a movie, having a piece of cake, buying a little present for yourself
